Atmospheric scientist with strong interests in **Python, air quality modeling, remote sensing, and environmental data analytics**.

## 🔬 Current Research and Projects

- 🛰️ **Satellite NO₂ and urban air quality in sub-Saharan Africa**

  Recent paper, *"Quantifying the monthly-scale temporal persistence of high-NO2 months in four sub-Saharan African cities using TROPOMI"*, is published in *Atmospheric Environment*:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atmosenv.2026.122525

  Code and data: https://github.com/Mekusgood/persistence-high-no2

- 🚀 **Air pollution forecasting**

  I am currently developing deep learning models, including **RNN-based approaches**, to forecast hourly air pollutant concentrations and support improved air quality modeling.

- 🌍 **Atmospheric Science and Black Carbon Research**

  My collaboration led to a peer-reviewed publication titled:

  **"City-produced and transported black carbon: Synergy of in-situ optical measurements and modeling."**

  The article is open access and available here:
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atmosres.2025.108731
